What is Design Detail?

Design Detail is a website builder made for car detailing businesses. Create and edit your website, showcase detailing packages and portfolio photos, add enquiry forms, and manage your site from one dashboard.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Use the visual editor to update your text, images, colours and layouts without writing code.

Can I connect my own domain?

Yes. Custom domain connection is included with Starter, Growth and Pro, with secure HTTPS hosting.

Is it suitable for mobile detailers and detailing studios?

Yes. Build a website around mobile valeting, studio detailing, ceramic coatings, paint correction or paint protection film.

Does Design Detail help with SEO?

Tools for page titles, meta descriptions and service content help explain what you do and where you work. Rankings depend on your content and competition.

Can I use widgets on my existing website?

Yes. Pricing tables, before-and-after comparisons, reviews and enquiry forms can be embedded into an existing website.
